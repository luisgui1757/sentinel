The reply is compressed. The opening answer may name the main points. After it, a sentence that only restates an earlier sentence, so that the reader can remove it with no loss of meaning or confidence, is a repeat. A list, table or section that restates another part is a repeat. A total, a consequence or a condition that prevents a wrong reading is not a repeat. It does not tell the story of the work. One short statement of what the agent read, ran or checked, and of what it did not check, is a required fact, not a step of the work. It lists no option that it does not recommend.
